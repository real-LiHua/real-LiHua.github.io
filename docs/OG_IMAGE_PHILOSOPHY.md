# Margin Signal

Space is an active material: a measured field of quiet, interrupted by one precise mark. Let forms gather like observations around an absence, leaving enough unoccupied ground for the eye to complete the thought. Every interval should feel deliberate, meticulously crafted, and resolved by someone with deep expertise.

Build from the cool neutrality of paper and graphite, then admit one red note with the authority of an annotation. Color should behave like evidence, not atmosphere; surfaces stay honest, edges crisp, and contrast carefully calibrated. The result must show painstaking attention at every scale, from the broad balance of the field to the smallest mark.

Let scale move from a restrained human voice to a larger instrument-like form. Repetition may suggest measurement or accumulated thought, but its rhythm should remain irregular enough to feel observed rather than mechanically tiled. Each line and interval should carry the patience of countless hours of refinement.

Composition is a conversation between a grounded left edge and a form that seems to continue beyond its own boundaries. A single point of emphasis holds the field together; everything else supports it without competing. Words are few, integrated as objects within the composition, while meaning travels chiefly through space, form, and visual rhythm.
